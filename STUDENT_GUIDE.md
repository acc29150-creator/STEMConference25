# Math 1710 AI Tutor - Student Guide

## Getting Started

1. **Enter your first name** (or first and last if you're a colleague)
2. **Select your unit** from the dropdown
3. **Choose your support level:**
   - **Quick Hints** - Fewer questions, larger steps
   - **Step-by-Step** - Guided practice with detailed support (recommended)
   - **Walk Me Through** - Maximum support with explanations
4. Click **Start Session**

---

## How the Tutor Works

### Answering Questions
- Every question has **4 multiple choice options** (A, B, C, D)
- **Option D is always "I'm not sure"** - pick this if you need a hint
- The tutor will guide you through problems step-by-step

### Getting Help
- **Pick "I'm not sure"** - Get a hint and try again
- **Type "hint"** - Get a helpful tip
- **Type "show me why"** - See a detailed explanation
- **Calculator button** - Use the built-in calculator anytime

### What to Expect
- **One question at a time** - Answer, then move to the next step
- **Work shown** - See all your progress like a whiteboard
- **Growth mindset** - The tutor encourages you when you're stuck

---

## Three Support Levels

| Mode | Best For | What You Get |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Quick Hints** | Students who want practice | Fewer questions, clean results, step summary at end |
| **Step-by-Step** | Most students | Guided practice with explanations |
| **Walk Me Through** | Need extra help or new to the topic | Detailed explanations for every step |

**Tip:** Start with Step-by-Step. You can always switch modes!

## Tips for Success

✓ **Read the question carefully** - Pick the best answer from A, B, C, or D

✓ **Use "I'm not sure" when stuck** - It's there to help you, not hurt you

✓ **Try before asking for hints** - You learn more by attempting first

✓ **Review the summary** - In Quick Hints mode, check the steps at the end

✓ **Ask "why" when confused** - Type "show me why" to understand the reasoning

✓ **Practice regularly** - Short sessions work better than cramming

## Common Questions

**Q: What if I get a question wrong?**
A: The tutor will give you a hint and let you try again. No penalties!

**Q: Can I switch modes during a problem?**
A: The tutor may switch you to more support if you're stuck. You can change modes between problems.

**Q: How do I know if my answer is right?**
A: The tutor accepts equivalent answers (like 0.5 and 1/2). If it's mathematically correct, you're good!

**Q: What if I need to stop and come back later?**
A: Just close the page. Start a new session when you're ready.
